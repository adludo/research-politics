# Victorian budget source links

Official sources from the Victorian Department of Treasury and Finance.

The [wiki budget overview](../../app/budgets/victoria/page.tsx) charts the ten latest completed financial years in the November 2026 workbook release. The repository keeps the chart values in `overview.json`.

Revenue, expenses and the published net result from transactions use column B, rows 27–36, of the `Total_revenue_GG`, `Total_expenses_GG` and `Net_Result_from_Transactions_GG` sheets. Net debt uses column B, rows 36–45, of `Net Debt - Gen Govt from 1987`. Match net debt by the calendar year in which the financial year ends. The underlying historical cells hold dollars. Divide by one billion for the chart. The workbooks note restatements and accounting changes.
